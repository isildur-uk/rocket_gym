Questions

Do I need any experience?

No. Every class is open to beginners unless the timetable says otherwise, and most of our members train for fun. If you would rather start quietly, ask for a taster session with a coach.

How does the free week work?

Send us a message, we suggest a class that suits, and you train free for 7 days. There is nothing to sign and no commitment at the end of it.

Is there a contract?

No contract and no hidden costs. Membership is monthly by direct debit and you can stop whenever you like.

Can I just use the gym?

Rocket is a private gym and the gym floor is kept for personal training clients. A small number of gym-only memberships are available at £35 a month, so ask us first.

Is this the same place as The Way Gym?

Yes. Rocket Gym was formerly The Way Gym. The coaches and the address have not changed.
